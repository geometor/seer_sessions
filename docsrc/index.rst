=============
SEER Sessions
=============

``geometor/seer_sessions`` is the empirical research archive of the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com/books/>`_ semantic digital archive. It preserves and analyzes 8,514 ARC-AGI reasoning trials across 14 benchmark suites, 475 session batches, and 2,939 unique puzzles—documenting how multi-agent program-synthesis topologies discover transformation invariants from visual demonstrations.
